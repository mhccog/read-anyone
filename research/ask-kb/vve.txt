## vve-blueprint | vve | ideas | The Conversation Blueprint
g: Conversation Blueprint; three levels
a: blueprint; levels of conversation; level 1 level 2 level 3; put people at ease connect deeply create meaning; go deeper in conversation
s: Vanessa's map for a good conversation has three levels, taken in order and at the other person's pace: Level 1 puts people at ease (light facts and fun), Level 2 connects more deeply (goals, values, worries), and Level 3 creates meaning (the story they tell about their life).
d: Each level has three power questions. You move up only when their answers get longer and warmer. If answers shrink, stay where you are or step back a level. Level 3 borrows from psychologist Dan McAdams's work on life stories.
u: Start light, listen for energy, then go one level deeper than feels comfortable — but only as fast as they go.
e: Level 1: "Working on anything exciting these days?" || Level 2: "What's your biggest goal right now?" || Level 3: "What would you do if you weren't afraid?"
