## ch-smalltalk | ch | smalltalk | The small-talk formula: Listen, Display, React, Ask, Offer
g: small-talk formula; small talk loop; LDRAO
a: small talk formula; small talk; listen display react ask offer; how to do small talk; small talk loop; keep a conversation going
s: Hughes's five-step loop: Listen properly; Display it on your face (react visibly to the high points); React with a few words; Ask one small follow-up question; then Offer a related story of your own. It matches research showing follow-up questions make people like you more.
d: His stated aim for small talk is that the other person leaves feeling interesting — not that you seem interesting.
u: Run the loop once in your next chat: listen, show it, react, ask, then offer.
e: They: "We just got back from Lisbon." You (face lights up): "Oh, nice!" (react) "What was the highlight?" (ask) …then later, "I went years ago and got lost for a day — best day of the trip." (offer)

## all-six-needs | ch | ideas | The six needs (listening prompts)

a: six needs; needs map; social needs; significance; approval; acceptance; intelligence; pity; hardship; power; strength; lean chips; what is lean; human needs
s: Hughes describes six social needs: significance, approval, acceptance, intelligence, pity (shown here as Hardship) and strength or power. On Read Anyone they're the Lean chips — listening prompts for the quiet question someone might be asking, never labels for people. Most people show a mix.
