## lo-overview | ch | leftout | What Read Anyone leaves out
a: excluded; left out; what is excluded; manipulation; manipulative techniques; dark psychology; covert; why exclude hughes
s: Read Anyone leaves out 41 techniques — 40 from Hughes and 1 from Van Edwards — because they don't pass the sincerity test. They include covert influence models, embedded commands, hypnosis, identity hacking, interrogation methods, information extraction with false statements, pressure through fears or needs, and lie-spotting from body language.
d: They're listed in the technique inventory so you can see what they are, but they never become lines. Where a technique has an honest core, the honest version is kept instead — for example, a sincere reaction statement instead of fishing statements, or a real "teach me" instead of pretending not to know.
u: Ask about any one of them and I'll say briefly what it is and point you to an honest alternative.
r: all-sincerity, lo-embedded, lo-lie-spotting
f: What is the sincerity test? || What are embedded commands?

g: embedded commands

## lo-lie-spotting | ch,vve | leftout | Lie-spotting from body language (left out)
