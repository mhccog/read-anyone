## vg-pause | vg | voice | The pause: replace the filler
g: pause; filler words; power pause
a: pause; pauses; um; uh; ah; filler words; like; silence; stop saying um; vinh pauses; pause guy
s: Vinh — who calls himself "the Pause Guy" — says don't fight your "um", replace it. When you feel a filler coming, pause instead: breathe, find your next thought, then carry on. A pause adds suspense, kills fillers, calms your body and lets people take in what you said.
d: Where to pause: before you answer a question (you sound more considered), before an opinion (more thoughtful), before a punchline (bigger laugh), and before and after a key word. u: Next time you're asked a question, pause for one breath before answering.
