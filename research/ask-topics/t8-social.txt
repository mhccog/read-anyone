n: vve-exit, all-end-on-purpose, vve-thinking-of-you, ch-gratitude, vve-connector
b: End on purpose, while it's still good — that's kinder than waiting for it to fizzle. Research by Mastroianni found most conversations don't end when either person wants them to, so you're probably doing them a favour. Vanessa's three-step exit: mention something in their future, give a specific compliment, then say what you're off to do. Chase adds a moment of thanks, which leaves a warm last impression. If you can, introduce them to someone else on your way out, so nobody's left standing alone.
say: "Good luck with the launch next week — I loved hearing about it. I'm going to grab a drink. Talk soon!"
tip: Smile, keep the energy up through the last line, and actually move — don't hover after you've said goodbye.
short: End on a high: mention their future, give a specific compliment, and say what you're off to do.
