## 1. Vanessa Van Edwards

### 1a. *Conversation* (2026) — the Conversation Blueprint

Three levels, taken in order and at the other person's pace: **L1 Put People at Ease** (general traits/facts), **L2 Connect Deeply** (goals, values, worries), **L3 Create Meaning** (self-narrative; borrowed from Dan McAdams). Three power questions per level.

## 3. Chase Hughes — *The Behavior Ops Manual* (primary), DOAC, NCI

Hughes's credentials and claims are disputed; none of his models has published validation. Everything below is **Coach's view** unless marked. The BOM is written for influence and interrogation, so far more of it is excluded than for the other two teachers.

## 4. How the inventory feeds the generator

- Every **USED** technique has at least two lines in `phrases.json` with `technique` set to the bold name above (short form), `teacher`-bearing source keys (`CONV`, `CAP`, `CUES` → Van Edwards; `BWF` → Giang; `BOM` → Hughes) and, where useful, a one-line `why` and a `delivery` tip.
- **DELIVERY** items feed the "How to say it" tips, not the words.
- **EXCLUDED** items never become lines. If Rapport is asked for them, it explains what they are and why they are left out.

## 5. Gaps and limits of this pass

- Chase: the BOM is the primary source. *The Ellipsis Manual* and *Six-Minute X-Ray* were read through published summaries only; NCI-1 to NCI-4 course content and the free masterclass are behind sign-up and were not seen.
- Vanessa: *Captivate*, *Cues* and *Conversation* were read in full. Not seen: People School course modules, the book "digital bonuses"/toolbox worksheets, her own podcast back catalogue (only captions and DOAC/Learning Leader summaries).
- Vinh: *Before We Forget How to Connect* read in full. Not seen: his paid programmes (the three courses sold on vinhgiang.com), his free email series, and most long-form YouTube without transcripts.
